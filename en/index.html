MYDISCARCHIVE

Catalog your records and CDs – with Discogs, on your phone and PC.

Your record collection, finally in order: MyDiscArchive manages vinyl, CDs and tapes – on your phone and on your Windows PC, with no cloud and no account with us.

Get MyDiscArchive

Features

BRING YOUR EXISTING COLLECTION

  • Import Excel and CSV lists, Discogs exports, CLZ and old CDPlus databases
  • The app looks up the matching pressing on Discogs for every record. Safe matches are linked automatically, uncertain ones go to a review list
  • Paste a Discogs link to assign a record precisely
  • Pressing assistant: all versions of an album side by side, with matrix numbers and label photos

ADD RECORDS FAST

  • Scan the barcode or take a photo of the cover – the app reads artist, title and catalog number
  • Batch scan: add many records in a row
  • Goldmine grading (M to P) for record and sleeve, with help for grading

EVERYTHING ABOUT EACH RECORD

  • Purchase price and date, dealer, shelf location
  • Market value by condition from Discogs, with your own Discogs account
  • History: every change can be undone

INSIGHTS AND PRINTING

  • Statistics: purchases per year, origins on a world map, favourites and fun facts
  • Lists to print or save as PDF
  • QR labels for sleeves and shelf compartments – scan them and the app shows the record or what's in the compartment

ON THE GO

  • Record fair mode: type or scan a catalog number and see at once whether you already own it
  • Wantlist with price alerts
  • Find gaps: which albums by your favourite artists are still missing

SELL AND CLEAR OUT

  • Create Discogs sale drafts with the correct pressing – you publish them yourself on Discogs
  • Help with clearing out a collection

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

  • All data is stored on your device. PC and phone sync directly over your home Wi-Fi
  • No ads, no account with us, no cloud
  • Backups and export to Excel and CSV at any time
  • Your collection works without internet; Discogs features need a connection

Available in 10 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Swedish and Japanese.
